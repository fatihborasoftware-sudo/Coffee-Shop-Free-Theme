A free block theme for coffee shops and roasters, ready for WooCommerce.

== Description ==

Navy, coffee brown, olive and rose palette with Anton headlines and Roboto body text (bundled, no Google requests).
Video hero with crossfading clips, tilted photo cards, scrolling outlined marquees, a product rail,
a cookie notice, a floating "Bayi ol" badge with a dealer popup, and a large footer with a newsletter band.
Templates for the home page, blog, posts, shop, product, cart and checkout pages, plus patterns
for the Neden Biz?, Bayilik and İletişim pages. Animations respect "reduce motion".

Recommended plugins: WooCommerce, Contact Form 7 (forms titled "İletişim" and "Bayilik başvurusu").

Filters: coffee_shop_hero_slides (array of extra hero image URLs), coffee_shop_loader,
coffee_shop_cookie_notice and coffee_shop_dealer_popup (return false to turn each off).
