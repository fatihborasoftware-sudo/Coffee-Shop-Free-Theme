A free block theme for coffee shops and roasters, ready for WooCommerce.

== Description ==

Dark ink and caramel palette, Roboto Slab headings and Roboto body text (bundled, no Google requests).
Includes templates for the home page, blog, posts, shop, product pages and a full width page template,
plus patterns for the Neden Biz?, Bayilik and İletişim pages.

Recommended plugins: WooCommerce, Contact Form 7.
